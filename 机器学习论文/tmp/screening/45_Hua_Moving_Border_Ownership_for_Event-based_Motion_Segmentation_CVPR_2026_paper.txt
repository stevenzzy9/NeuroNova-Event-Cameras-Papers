first define the event stream and windowing conventions
(Sec. 3.1), describe the input representation (Sec. 3.2), spec-
ify the ground-truth labels for supervision (Sec. 3.3), de-
tail the network architecture (Sec. 3.4), introduce LoRA for
bridging the synthetic-to-real domain gap (Sec. 8.1), and fi-
nally define the training objectives (Sec. 3.6).
