Figure 2. The overall framework of our proposed POKER enhances feature discriminability via polarity-aware motion knowledge. It
consists of event and text encoders augmented by Polarity Motion Capturer (PMC) and Polarity Motion Reasoner (PMR). During training,
the encoders process two distinct data streams: one for generating base features from the original event frames and text prompts, and another
for generating polarity-enhanced features from the PMC’s decoupled visual streams and the PMR’s generated reasoning descriptions. These
base and enhanced features are jointly optimized through contrastive learning and a polarity alignment loss to achieve robust cross-modal
coupling. Critically, inference relies solely on the base features for final action prediction.
