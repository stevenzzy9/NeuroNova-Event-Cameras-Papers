polarities and processed by early aggregation. The inputs are then fed into the Spiking Discrete Aggregation (SDA) module, which employs
gated recurrent spiking neurons. SDA produces the representation that can be seamlessly integrated into a fully spiking detector. B. SDA
with Multi-Timescale Fusion. The top part shows the architecture, where an additional timescale branch is incorporated based on the
vanilla SDA. The bottom part presents a more detailed and intuitive view of the computation. C. Dynamics of Gated Recurrent Spiking
Neuron. The red solid lines indicate the recurrent connections while the blue and black solid lines depict the input pathways and internal
dynamics, respectively. R and F indicate the reset and forget gate.
